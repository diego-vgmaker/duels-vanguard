Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: you can play without an account and nothing leaves your browser. If you create an account we keep your email, your player name and the deck lists you finish matches with. Statistics shown to others never include your name or email. You can delete everything at any time.

Playing without an account

Decks, settings, your local rating, match history and any card images you import are stored only in your browser (local storage). They are never sent to us.

What we store when you have an account

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password, or, if you continue with Google, the email and name Google shares with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Player name: the name you choose, which other players can see.
  • Finished matches: for each match that lasts at least three turns, the cards in the deck you used and how many copies, its nation, its grade 3 vanguard, the result, the number of turns and the date. Your saved decks themselves stay in your browser.

How it is used

Only to sign you in, show your player name and build the public Meta page, which shows totals such as how often a nation, vanguard or card is played. Those totals never reveal who played what. There are no ads, no trackers and nothing is sold.

Services involved

  • Supabase hosts the accounts and the database.
  • Google handles sign-in when you choose "Continue with Google".
  • GitHub Pages serves the website, jsDelivr and cdnjs serve code libraries, and Google Fonts serves the fonts. Like any web host they may see your IP address when your browser loads files.
  • Card images are loaded from the official Cardfight!! Vanguard website.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete it, sign in, open your account menu at the top of the page and choose Delete account: your account, player name and recorded matches are removed for good. Totals already shown on the Meta page are recalculated without them.

Your rights

Under the Brazilian LGPD, the European GDPR and similar laws you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Most of this you can do yourself from the account menu; for anything else, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
